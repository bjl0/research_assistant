What the figures mean

  • Age counts from when the token or its pool was first seen, whichever is earlier.
  • Top-10 wallets is the share of supply held by the ten largest holders, leaving out liquidity pools and token lockers.
  • Largest wallet is the biggest single holder that is not a pool or locker. It can be the creator.
  • Locked liquidity is the share of pooled liquidity RugCheck counts as locked or burned.
  • Identified holders are pools, lockers and the creator as RugCheck labels them, plus the pump.fun profile name of large wallets where one exists. A profile name is a self-chosen handle, not a verified identity.
  • X links are whatever the token's team listed on DEX Screener. One marked "X post" or "X community" is not the token's own account, and a listed post can belong to someone unrelated.
  • Linked wallets are wallets RugCheck's network analysis ties together. A flag appears from twenty.
  • Near-identical amounts means five or more top wallets within three percent of each other, which can be one buyer split across wallets.

Limits of this screen

  • Candidates come from tokens whose teams filled in a DEX Screener profile or paid for a boost. That is what is being marketed, not the whole market.
  • Holder checks run only on tokens that pass the saved age, liquidity and volume limits, and never below $10,000 of pooled liquidity. The rest show "Not checked" and cannot meet the strategy.
  • Wallet names are looked up for holders of at least one percent, a few per run, so new alerts fill in over the next runs.
  • Holder data covers the largest holders only. It cannot tell when one person spreads supply across many wallets.
  • Alerts arrive hourly at best. A launch can rise and collapse between two runs.
  • Market data is from DEX Screener, holder and safety data from RugCheck, profile names from pump.fun.
